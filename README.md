## About

**Design saves lives.** Safety, income and stability are at the forefront of how we operate our daily lives. We rely on systems to survive, and poorly designed infrastructure can cause immense harm. It's sad, but also interestingly optimistic, that a lot of these problems are preventable.

I find parts of those experiences and identify what breaks in them. A lot of our infrastructure still runs on low-cost, legacy systems with fragmented, outdated usability. Where does the flow break for the user? Is the layout right for them? Could color, scale, typography, iconography or shape be the reason people take longer to navigate a system, or leave it?

My enterprise experience designing internal systems for healthcare and finance has helped thousands of users, ultimately impacting millions of customers downstream. I've made systems faster and clearer, centralized fragmented ones, and emphasized safety, clarity and security.

I started off as an artist, drawing obsessively as a kid, and in trying to depict the human condition I noticed that everything is rooted in systems. At heart I'm a creative who likes finding depth and clarity inside complex, chaotic environments, and I've stopped thinking of design as a compromise between users and businesses: when customers and employees move through systems with less friction, the business usually benefits too. That's how I see design now, not just as something visual, but as something systemic.

## How this portfolio is built

The site is treated as a product: it was iterated against real constraints (a laptop screen, an ultrawide monitor, a phone, a dark-mode OS, a keyboard, reduced motion), and changes are measured in a browser before they ship.

- **No framework, no build step.** One hand-written `index.html` with its CSS inline, vanilla JavaScript in `assets/app-1.js` and `assets/app-2.js`, content-hashed images, and fonts self-hosted so the page makes no runtime requests to other origins. Served by GitHub Pages from this repository.
- **The landing page is a sequence.** A conversation plays first, centered on the screen; *See the work* opens the rest of the portfolio around it.
- **Project visuals are built, not screenshotted** where it helps: the LiveFront and Scale cards draw their phone screens in HTML/CSS, and several case-study previews are live embedded artifacts.
- **Light by default**, with a site-controlled dark mode; `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected throughout.
- **Tested in the repo.** `tools/check.py` drives headless Chrome to check routes, theme, the landing sequence at fifteen widths, keyboard order and copy; `tools/buildslop.py` generates the `/slop/` page.

The implementation is AI-assisted (Claude Code). Framing, interaction logic, evidence, accessibility and product decisions stay mine.
